Erase this supporter

Erasure rewrites the name, email, telephone, address and Stripe customer with placeholders, keeps only the year of birth, drops the email index and cancels any live subscription. Orders, ticket numbers and wins stay in the gambling ledger. It is refused while a prize is still pending or notified.

Dates are UTC and must run opens before closes, closes on or before the draw, and the draw on or before the results. The ticket price cannot change once a ticket is sold, and the cap cannot drop below the number already sold.

Saving a rank replaces whatever tier held it. Pick a rank in the table to load it. Tiers are frozen once the raffle is drawn.

Draw ·

The draw is claimed once and recorded with who conducted it and who witnessed it. Numbers come from operating-system entropy; refunded tickets and tickets that have already won are skipped.

Run the draw

A second run resumes an interrupted draw from the winners already recorded. It never re-picks a prize that has been awarded.
